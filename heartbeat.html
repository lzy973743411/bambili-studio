01 — About the Project

Music as identity

A social app that lets you find people nearby based on what you're listening to — not how you look.

"Heartbeat connects music enthusiasts nearby who share your taste. The emphasis is on music, not appearance — helping those who struggle with traditional photo-based social platforms find their people."

02 — Problems

Photo-first isn't for everyone

The current social landscape is built around appearance. That excludes a lot of people.

App Screen Placeholder
1

Social media primarily revolves around photo-sharing, making identity visual by default.

2

Individuals lacking confidence in their appearance may feel excluded or uncomfortable.

3

Users who prefer not to share photos find mainstream platforms less appealing or safe.

4

Privacy concerns around photo theft and identity remain unaddressed on most platforms.

04 — Solutions

Connect through sound

Four design principles that shift the focus from looks to listening.

♪

Music-first matching

Connect with nearby users based on shared musical preferences. The emphasis is on music, not appearance.

◎

Genre-based discovery

Discover like-minded individuals through musical genres and compositions, fostering friendships through shared taste.

✦

Low-barrier conversation

Assist those who struggle with verbal expression by giving them music as the opening line of any conversation.

◇

Appearance-free identity

Shift the focus away from physical appearances — encouraging users to appreciate each other for musical interests.
