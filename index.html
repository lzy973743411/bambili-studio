About

Zhiyi (Bambi) Li · New York

From drawing, to operations, to AI.

I'm a product designer who likes the rules underneath the screen as much as the screen itself.

I trained in illustration and animation at the Fashion Institute of Technology. After graduating, I designed for a restaurant-tech company and a media group, then joined a mortgage lending firm as a graphic designer. Much of that job was spent alongside loan processors, turning underwriting conditions into step-by-step guidance borrowers could follow, and building a shared tracker so the team could see what each file still needed. It taught me that a lot of confusion comes from rules nobody has made visible.

Since then I've designed booking, POS and an AI phone line for Glowin, a B2B platform for salons and restaurants, and built Tossy on my own with AI coding tools, from first sketch to a live product.

What connects all of it is how people decide: what to choose, what to do next, who to talk to. That is what I want to keep studying and designing for.
