About

Zhiyi (Bambi) Li · New York

From drawing, to operations, to AI.

I'm a product designer who likes the rules underneath the screen as much as the screen itself.

I trained in illustration and animation at the Fashion Institute of Technology. After graduating, I designed for a restaurant-tech company and a media group, then joined a mortgage lending firm as a graphic designer, where I also built a shared tracker so loan processors and officers could see what each file still needed.

Since then I've designed booking, POS and an AI phone line for Glowin, a B2B platform for salons and restaurants, and built Tossy on my own with AI coding tools, from first sketch to a live product.

What connects all of it is the layer under the screen: the rules that decide what people see, what they can do next, and who gets to choose. That is what I want to keep studying and designing for.
